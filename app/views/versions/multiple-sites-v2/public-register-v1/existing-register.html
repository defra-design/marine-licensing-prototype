Public register

The Marine Management Organisation maintains a register containing the particulars prescribed in the Marine Licensing (Register of Licence Information) Regulations 2011. This information is held under Section 101 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009.

We publicise up-to-date information of:

  • Marine licence applications and decisions, including marine minerals extraction and renewable energy documents
  • Environmental impact assessment documents, including screening and scoping opinions and environmental impact assessment consent decisions

We are introducing a new public register for marine licence applications and decisions. We have started publishing some types of application on it.
