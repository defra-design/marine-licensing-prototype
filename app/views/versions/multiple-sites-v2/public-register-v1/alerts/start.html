Get email alerts

Get an email when we publish a new marine licence application in an area you are interested in.

You choose:

  • the marine plan areas you want alerts for
  • the types of work, if you only want some of them – construction, deposit or removal

We will email you when a matching application is first published on this register. We will not email you when an application changes or a decision is made.

You do not need an account. We will send you an email to confirm your alert before it starts.
