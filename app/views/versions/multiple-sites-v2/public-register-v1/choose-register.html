Which public register do you need?

We are introducing a new public register for marine licence applications and decisions. We have started publishing some types of application on it. While we change over, we have 2 public registers: our existing one and our new one.

Use our existing public register

Applications received before [date], and other types of application received since, are on our existing public register. You can comment on applications that are open for consultation.

Use our new public register

Some types of application received since [date] are on our new public register. You can comment on applications that are open for consultation.

Get email alerts

Get an email when we publish a new application on our new public register, in an area or for a type of activity you’re interested in.

Set up an email alert

About the public register

The Marine Management Organisation (MMO) keeps the public register under section 101 of the Marine and Coastal Access Act 2009 and the Marine Licensing (Register of Licence Information) Regulations 2011.

We publish information about each application, including the applicant’s name and address.
