You must publish one or more site notices to tell people about your application. This gives people who may be affected by, or interested in, the proposed activity the chance to comment.

Notices should be placed in locations chosen to best bring the proposed application to the attention of the public and interested parties. Consider locations such as those below.

Marine users

This means people who use the water nearby, such as boat users, sailors, or fishing vessels. Prominent locations for this group include:

  • harbour noticeboards
  • port noticeboards
  • marinas
  • yacht clubs
  • fishing clubs

Community users

This means people nearby on land, such as residents and visitors. Prominent locations for this group include:

  • beach access points
  • coastal path access points
  • community noticeboards
  • visitor information points
{% if multipleSites %}

Multiple sites

If your application covers multiple sites, check whether your chosen locations are enough to reach interested parties at each one.

You may need extra notice locations if your sites are spread across different beaches, harbours, ports or coastal communities.

{% endif %}

Evidence you must provide

For each site notice you publish, you must provide:

  • one close-up photograph clearly showing the notice
  • one photograph showing the notice in its surrounding location, showing where it has been displayed
  • the date the notice was first displayed

When to display your notice

You must display your notice within 7 days of receiving this notification, then provide your evidence as soon as possible.

Once displayed, your notice must remain in place for 21 days.

How we manage consultation responses

We will review any responses we receive and let you know:

  • a summary of the responses we received
  • whether you need to take any action, and what this involves
  • a deadline for responding, if action is needed
  • what happens if you do not respond
  • any measures you could take to address the issues raised

We aim to give you this in one update, so you do not need to be contacted more than once.

Get your site notice

Print the notice and display it at each location. Most of it is already filled in with your application details. You only need to write in the date when you first display it.
