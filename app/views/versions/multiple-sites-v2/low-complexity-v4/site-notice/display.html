MLA/2026/10020 – Dawlish sea defence extension

Display a site notice

You must publicise your proposed marine activity for 21 days so that people who may be affected by it, or interested in it, can comment during the consultation period.

Site notices must be displayed where people are most likely to see them. This means near where the proposed activity is taking place, and in your local community.

Marine users

These are people who use the marine environment, such as boat users, sailors, or fishing operators. Places to display a notice for this group include:

  • harbour noticeboards
  • port noticeboards
  • marinas
  • yacht clubs
  • fishing clubs

Community users

These are people who live near or visit the area, such as residents and visitors. Places to display a notice for this group include:

  • beach access points
  • coastal path and access points
  • community noticeboards
  • slipways and water sports clubs
{% if multipleSites %}

Multiple sites

Your application covers more than one site. You must display a notice at each site, and tell us about each one separately below.

If your sites are close together and share the same noticeboards, you can display one notice that covers them all. Tell us which sites it covers when you send us your evidence.

{% endif %}

Evidence you must provide

For each site notice you put up, you must provide:

  • a close-up photograph clearly showing the notice
  • a photograph showing the notice in its surroundings, so we can see where it is displayed
  • the date you first displayed the notice

Site notices must remain in place for the whole 21 days. You must send us this evidence before we can make a decision on your application.
