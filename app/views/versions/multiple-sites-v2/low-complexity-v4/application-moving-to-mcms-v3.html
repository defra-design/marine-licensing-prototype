Your application is moving to a different case management system

We are moving your application (MLA/2026/10003) to the Marine Case Management System (MCMS). This will not affect how we consider or make a decision on your application.

Why your application is moving

We are changing the systems we use to handle marine licence applications. While we do this, we handle some applications here and others in MCMS.

Yours needs to be handled in MCMS.

What you need to do now

You need an MCMS account. We cannot move your application to MCMS until you have one.

If you do not already have one, create an MCMS account now.

If you already have an MCMS account, you do not need to do anything.

What we will do

Once you have an MCMS account, we will use the information you have entered to recreate your application in MCMS, including any documents you have uploaded. This can take up to 3 working days.

MCMS asks for information in a different way to this system, so your application will not look the same. You will probably need to answer more questions.

We will contact you when your draft application is ready for you to review and submit.

If you want to refer to what you entered, you can view your submitted application in this system.

The status of your application will show as 'Moving to MCMS' until we have moved it.
