We are required by law to publish information about your application on the MMO's public register.

The information may also appear on Explore Marine Plans (opens in new tab).

Publishing this information helps you and other applicants:

  • get a clear picture of activity in the marine environment
  • prepare applications more effectively by avoiding conflicts with existing marine activity
  • evaluate the environmental impact of proposed works in the context of other marine activity, leading to better applications and evidence for decision makers

We will not publish contact details, such as your email address, phone number or postal address, or any financial information.

Requesting information is withheld

You can only ask us to withhold specific information from the public register if publishing it would:

  • compromise national security, or
  • breach a legally protected commercial or industrial confidentiality

We will not withhold information for any other reasons.

You must justify your request. We will review it and decide whether the information can be withheld.
