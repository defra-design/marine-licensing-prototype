This applies where publishing would harm a legitimate commercial interest that is protected by law. It may include:

  • trade secrets
  • proprietary methods, techniques or processes
  • intellectual property
  • commercial agreements and contractual arrangements
  • pricing structures, cost models or financial information
  • future commercial plans, strategies or investment decisions
  • sensitive operational or technical information that gives a competitive advantage

The information must not already be publicly available, and must be subject to a legally recognised duty of confidentiality.

Publishing it would need to cause real harm that you can identify. Wanting to keep it private, being open to public scrutiny or worrying about your reputation is not enough.
