## Applicant's request on the Dawlish view details page (user testing only, commit 55eba161)

Shown under Sharing your application information publicly:

- Request that information is withheld: Yes
- Details of information to be withheld and why:

> We would like two things withheld because they are commercially confidential. First, the unit rates table in section 4 of our method statement. These are the prices we have agreed with our contractor and suppliers, and publishing them would let competitors undercut us on future coastal defence tenders. Second, the vessel movement schedule in appendix B. It shows how we plan and sequence our marine plant, which we have developed over several projects. If this information were published, other contractors could use it to price against us and we could lose future work.
